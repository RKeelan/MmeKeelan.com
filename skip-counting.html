Skip Counting — Worksheet Generator

Generate printable number charts from 1 to 100, each headed by the number to count by, for the student to circle every number the count lands on. Leave the skip counts blank for a random one from 2 to 9 on every page, give one for every page to share, or give a list like 2, 5, 10 for a page each. Colouring the columns makes the pattern of the count easier to see.
